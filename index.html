Shomen

A private bonsai journal: photos from the same front, wire check reminders, styling plans and a season calendar.

Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Write to jonas.volk89@gmail.com – we usually answer within two working days.

To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

To move your trees to a new iPhone, use Settings → Save a backup (Shomen Pro) and restore the file on the new device, or use an iCloud backup of your iPhone.

About the care hints

The season calendar and the hints in Shomen are general guidelines for healthy trees in a typical climate. Weather, the state of your tree, the species and your local conditions always come first. Shomen does not recommend plant protection products or fertiliser amounts; always follow the label of the product you use.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Shomen ("the app") is developed by Jonas Volk, Switzerland. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

No data collection

Shomen does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter – trees, photos, wires, plans, work entries, soil mixes and notes – is stored only on your device. If you use iCloud device backups, this data may be included in your backup under Apple's privacy policy. You can delete all data in the app (Settings → Delete all data); deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Camera and photos

The camera is only used when you take a photo of a tree. Photos you take or choose from your library are stored inside the app on your device. Shomen only saves to your photo library when you ask it to (for example a time-lapse video).

Exports

Tree passports (PDF), time-lapse videos and backup files are created on your device. They only leave your device if you choose to share or save them, and then only to the place or recipient you pick.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, wire check and monthly reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Apple shares only anonymous, aggregated sales information with developers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
